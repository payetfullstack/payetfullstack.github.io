Features

Two tools. One hand.

Everything you need before and during practice, and nothing you don't.

Guitar tuner

  • Precise real-time pitch detection, reliable down to the low E string
  • Clear instructions: Tighten, Loosen or In tune
  • Large cents gauge (−50 to +50) with red, yellow and green feedback
  • Auto mode detects which string you're playing, or pick one yourself
  • Adjustable reference pitch, A4 = 430–450 Hz
  • The screen stays on while you tune

Metronome

  • 30–300 BPM with a slider, +/− buttons, or type the exact tempo
  • Tap tempo
  • Time signatures 2/4, 3/4, 4/4, 5/4, 6/8, 7/8 and custom
  • Quarter notes, eighths, triplets and sixteenths
  • Accented first beat, three click sounds and volume control
  • Remembers your last settings

Under the hood

Made for the practice room

Timing that never drifts

Clicks are placed by the audio engine, sample by sample, not by the screen. Change the tempo while it plays and it carries on from the same beat.

Keeps playing in your pocket

Lock the screen or switch apps and it keeps counting, with pause and stop in the notification. It pauses for phone calls and when you unplug your headphones.

Private by design

The microphone is used only by the tuner, and only while it's open. Sound is analysed on your phone and never recorded or uploaded. No account, no sign-up.
