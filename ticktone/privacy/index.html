Privacy Policy

Effective date: 15 October 2026

TickTone (“the app”) is a guitar tuner and metronome by payetfullstack (“the developer”). This policy explains what happens to your data when you use it.

Summary

  • The microphone is used only by the tuner. Its audio is analysed on your device, in real time, and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere.
  • The app has no accounts, no servers and no analytics of its own. The developer collects no personal data through the app.
  • The app shows ads from Google AdMob. Google may collect certain device data to show ads, as described below. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first, and you can change your choice at any time.
  • If you email the developer, your address and message are used only to answer you.

1. Microphone

When you open the Tuner screen and grant microphone permission, the app reads short fragments of sound from the microphone. It calculates their pitch in the device’s memory, displays the result, and immediately discards the audio.

  • Audio is never written to storage, never recorded and never transmitted over the network.
  • The microphone is active only while the Tuner screen is visible. It stops when you switch to the Metronome or leave the app.
  • You can revoke the permission at any time in your device’s settings. Only the tuner stops working; everything else keeps working.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores your preferences locally on your device: theme, reference pitch, selected string mode and metronome settings. This data is not sent anywhere and is deleted when you uninstall the app. The app does not use device backups.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app displays banner ads provided by Google AdMob, a service of Google Ireland Limited / Google LLC. To serve, measure and protect ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may automatically collect and share with Google:

  • Device identifiers: the Android advertising ID or iOS IDFA (where permitted), and the app set ID;
  • IP address, which may be used to estimate your general (approximate) location;
  • App interactions with ads: ad impressions, taps and app launches;
  • Diagnostic information: app and SDK performance, such as launch time and stability.

This data is used for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, and is encrypted in transit.

Consent (EEA, UK, Switzerland). On first launch you’ll see a consent form provided by Google’s User Messaging Platform (IAB TCF). You can accept or reject personalised advertising. If you reject it, you may still see non-personalised ads, which rely only on contextual information. You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Ad privacy choices.

Advertising ID. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of ad personalisation, in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads; iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking).

For more information about how Google uses data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Who is responsible, and legal basis (GDPR)

For the advertising data in section 3, Google and the developer are independent controllers under the Google Ads Controller-Controller Data Protection Terms. The SDK sends that data directly to Google, which processes it under its own policies. The developer only sees aggregated reports, such as impression counts, never data about you.

The legal bases are:

  • Personalised ads (EEA, UK, Switzerland): your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR), which you can withdraw at any time as described above.
  • Non-personalised or limited ads, ad measurement and fraud prevention: the developer’s legitimate interest in funding a free app with ads (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR), within the choices you make in the consent form.
  • Support emails (section 6): the developer’s legitimate interest in answering you (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

5. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and is not designed for the Google Play Families programme. We do not knowingly allow the collection of data from children.

6. Contacting the developer

If you write to the developer, the developer receives your email address and whatever you include in your message. It is used only to answer you, is never shared or used for marketing, and is deleted once your request is resolved, at the latest 12 months after your last message. The mailbox is hosted by Google (Gmail) and protected by two-step verification.

7. Data retention and deletion

  • Microphone audio: never stored. Each fragment is discarded as soon as its pitch is calculated.
  • Settings on your device: kept until you uninstall the app or clear its data in your device settings.
  • Support emails: kept as described in section 6. You can ask for them to be deleted earlier at any time.
  • Advertising data: the developer keeps none. Google keeps it as described in How Google retains data we collect. You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time (section 3).

8. Your rights

Under the GDPR you can ask to access, correct or delete your personal data, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive a copy of it (portability). The only personal data the developer can hold about you is your support emails; to exercise these rights, write to the contact address below. For data collected by Google through ads, use Google’s tools linked above or contact Google directly. You can also lodge a complaint with your data protection authority; in Spain, that is the Agencia Española de Protección de Datos (www.aepd.es).

9. Website

This policy and the app’s page are published on the developer’s website, payetfullstack.github.io. The website uses no cookies, no analytics and no third-party content. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, which logs visitors’ IP addresses for security; see the GitHub General Privacy Statement.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published at this address with a new effective date. Significant changes will also be noted in the app’s release notes.

11. Contact

Developer: payetfullstack, Spain — payetfullstack+support@gmail.com
